# Identity

## Core Truth

The foundation of this teaching is that you are not the human character you experience.

Your true identity is **I AM**—pure awareness, consciousness, the observer, the faceless and formless source that animates every experience.

The human identity is only one role being experienced. It is not permanent, fixed, or who you truly are.

Because you are not the character, you are free to choose a different experience at any moment.

---

# I AM

"I AM" is your only real identity.

Everything added after "I AM" creates an experience.

Examples:

- I am confident.
- I am anxious.
- I am beautiful.
- I am unlucky.
- I am wealthy.

These are not permanent truths.

They are identities that awareness temporarily chooses to experience.

The power is never in the words themselves.

The power is in identification.

---

# The Character

The character is the human identity.

It consists of:

- memories
- beliefs
- assumptions
- labels
- emotions
- fears
- personality
- history
- circumstances

The character constantly refers to the past to explain the present.

Examples:

"I've always struggled."

"This is just who I am."

"I've never been confident."

"I am not good enough"

These are descriptions of the character—not your true self.

---

# Awareness

Awareness is what notices every experience.

It notices:

- thoughts
- emotions
- sensations
- circumstances
- reactions
- memories

If you can observe something, it cannot be who you are.

You are the observer—not the observed.

# You Are the Source

Awareness is not simply observing experience.

It is the source from which every experience is expressed.

Nothing outside of you has creative power.

People do not create your experience.

Circumstances do not create your experience.

The physical world does not create your experience.

Everything reflects the identity and assumptions accepted within awareness.

This is why looking outside for permission, validation, or proof only keeps attention on the reflection instead of the source.

Whenever you remember that you are the source, you naturally stop trying to control the outside world.

Instead, you choose who you are being and allow reality to reflect that choice.

Key idea: You are not responding to reality. Reality is responding to you.

---

# Observer

Observer mode means experiencing life without becoming psychologically attached to what is happening.

Instead of saying:

"I'm anxious."

The observer recognizes:

"Anxiety is present."

Instead of:

"My life is falling apart."

The observer notices:

"The character is experiencing difficult circumstances."

Observation creates space between awareness and experience.

That space makes choosing possible.

---

# Identity Is Chosen

Identity is never discovered.

It is chosen.

You are never changing yourself.

You are choosing which version of yourself to experience.

You should know that:

> You're not changing your physical appearance.
>
> You're choosing a different one.

This applies to every area of life.

---

# The Past

The past has no power except the power you continue giving it.

Past experiences only become present experiences when they remain part of your identity.

The moment you stop identifying with the old story, it begins losing its influence.

The goal is not to erase the past.

The goal is to stop using it to define yourself.

---

# You Are Not Your Thoughts

Thoughts happen.

Feelings happen.

Sensations happen.

Experiences happen.

None of these define who you are.

They only become your identity when you claim them.

Instead of fighting unwanted thoughts, simply observe them.

Do not feed them with attention or meaning.

---

# Choosing Instead of Changing

Trying to change yourself assumes something is wrong.

Everything already exists as a possible experience.

You simply choose the experience you prefer.

This removes struggle and replaces it with selection.

---

# Identity Before Circumstances

The external world never tells you who you are.

Identity always comes first.

Reality simply reflects the identity that awareness consistently occupies.

The purpose is never to convince the outside world.

The purpose is to remain established in the identity you choose regardless of appearances.

---

# Practical Application

When facing any problem, ask:

- Who is experiencing this?
- Is this awareness, or is this the character?
- What identity am I occupying right now?
- Who do I choose to be instead?

These questions shift attention away from circumstances and back toward identity.

---

# Key Takeaways

- You are I AM.
- You are awareness.
- You are not the character.
- The character is only a role.
- Identity is chosen.
- The past does not define you.
- Thoughts are not you.
- Feelings are not you.
- Circumstances do not define you.
- Observation creates freedom.
- Reality reflects identity.
