# Applications

This file explains how the core principles are applied to different areas of life.

Although the subjects appear different, every application follows the same process:

* Recognize that you are the source.
* Choose the identity that naturally has the experience.
* Be that identity now.
* Allow reality to reflect it.

The application changes.

The principles never do.

---

## Physical Appearance

Physical appearance is not changed by fighting the current appearance.

The current appearance simply reflects the identity being occupied.

Trying to change individual features keeps attention on the version considered “wrong.”

Instead:

* Stop identifying with the current appearance.
* Know yourself as the source.
* Occupy the version of yourself that naturally loves the way they look.
* Appreciate yourself now instead of waiting for physical proof.
* Let the body reflect that identity without forcing it.

The goal is never perfect features.

The goal is becoming the version of yourself that already experiences the appearance you prefer.

---

## Money

Money is not created through need.

Need confirms lack.

Instead of trying to become someone who has money, become the version for whom abundance is already normal.

Focus on experiences such as:

* freedom
* security
* ease
* generosity
* enoughness

Stop asking how money will come.

Remain established in the identity that already experiences financial freedom.

Money becomes one natural reflection of that identity.

---

## Relationships

Relationships reflect the assumptions you hold about yourself and others.

Do not attempt to change another person.

Instead ask:

* Who am I being?
* What version of me naturally experiences love, commitment, and respect?

If the current identity expects rejection, abandonment, inconsistency, or separation, those assumptions continue to be reflected.

Choose the version of yourself for whom healthy relationships are simply normal.

---

## Health

Health is experienced differently when attention moves away from illness as an identity.

This does not mean denying symptoms or ignoring appropriate care.

It means refusing to define yourself by what the body is currently expressing.

Observe symptoms without building an identity around them.

Know yourself as the source rather than the condition.

Health becomes a natural reflection of the identity you occupy.

---

## Career

Stop identifying as someone trying to become successful.

Instead ask:

* What would the successful version naturally think?
* What decisions would they make?
* What opportunities would feel normal?
* How would they see themselves?

Occupy that identity first.

Allow career opportunities to reflect it.

---

## Self-Concept

Everything ultimately returns to identity.

Changing self-concept changes every area because every experience reflects who you believe yourself to be.

There is no need to solve every problem separately. Transform the identity behind the problems, and many areas of life will naturally begin to change.

Choose a different identity.

Everything else naturally reorganizes around it.
